The liquidation of Struckd: How the corporation Unity destroyed a childhood dream

A timeline of systemic neglect, corporate resource extraction, and the tragic shutdown of a global community of young developers.

«Struckd wasn't just a platform. It was a place where childhood dreams of making your first game came true.»

Imagine a child realizing for the first time that they can create their own worlds. Hundreds of thousands of children and teenagers around the world opened Struckd every day after school. They didn't know complex programming, but they had the most important thing — imagination. Struckd taught them to believe in themselves, find their first friends, share their creativity, and be proud of the virtual worlds they built.

Unity Technologies' cold corporate calculus erased all those years. Without pity or any chance of rescue, millions of hours of children's labor, their first sincere joy, and the pride of young authors turned into digital dust. This isn't just a shutdown — it's a betrayal of an entire generation's trust.

1. Official Struckd Statement

Below is the official message from the Struckd administration to the community, published before the final server shutdown:

Official Struckd Statement
«Over the years, Struckd has become something truly special. What started as a creative platform has grown into a global community of creators and players. Even today, many of you continue to create, share, and play every month — thousands of new games are created daily. This community creativity has always been at the heart of what we do and continued to inspire us every day. That is why making this decision has been incredibly difficult. With great regret, we announce that Struckd will officially close on August 1, 2026. As of August 1, 2026, the Struckd platform (including the app and website) will become unavailable. You will no longer be able to log in, play, create new games, or access previously published content. We are deeply grateful to each of you. Your passion and support made Struckd a place where millions of ideas came to life. Thank you for being part of this journey with us. Sincerely, The Struckd Team»

2. Investigation: Unity Technologies' Negligence

The acquisition of the platform by Unity Technologies in 2021 promised a technological breakthrough, but in practice it led to the slow degradation and destruction of the project.

Key factors and timeline of corporate failure:
  • Resource and talent drain: After purchasing the Swiss studio, Unity redirected Struckd's lead developers to internal corporate projects, leaving the platform without its core engineering team.
  • Ignoring critical bugs: Throughout 2023–2025, the server infrastructure suffered constant outages, but technical support tickets were closed with automated responses.
  • Complete lack of monetization strategy: Unity failed to provide a viable development model for creators, while simultaneously blocking third-party integration opportunities.
  • Destruction of user archives: The community was given no tools to locally save their worlds, maps, or 3D models, turning millions of user projects into Lost Media.

3. Historical timeline and lost heritage (Lost Media)

2016
Platform founding

Flurin Jenal and Silvan Bauser launch Struckd in Switzerland as an intuitive 3D game builder for mobile devices.

December 2017
Special projects and collaborations Lost Media

Limited-time events and asset packs, such as Struckd & YR Christmas and branded Struckd Audi integrations, are now completely lost.

2021
Deal with Unity Technologies

Unity acquires Struckd. A period of explosive audience growth begins, later cut short by funding cuts.

August 1, 2026
Final liquidation Lost Media

Servers are shut down. The database containing millions of unique user-generated games is permanently deleted without any possibility of recovery.

4. Community initiative: The ReStruckd Project

Reviving the legendary game builder

The community of enthusiasts and independent developers has announced the start of work on **ReStruckd** — an open-source non-commercial alternative to the original platform.

Project tech stack:
The server side is being designed using distributed databases (Neon PostgreSQL) and modern cloud services (Railway). The architecture is being developed with a focus on preserving user assets, offline mode, and complete independence from large corporations.
